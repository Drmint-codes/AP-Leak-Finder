01 of 08 · Paid twice, or too much

Exact duplicate payment

The same invoice from the same vendor was paid more than once.

  • Same vendor
  • Same invoice
  • Same amount
02 of 08 · Paid twice, or too much

Near-duplicate payment

The same amount went to the same vendor within 45 days, on a different invoice number. It might be a real second bill, or the same one entered twice.

  • Same vendor
  • Same amount
  • Different invoice
03 of 08 · Paid twice, or too much

Overpayment

A payment came in above the amount on its invoice.

  • Matches an invoice
  • Paid more than invoiced
04 of 08 · Worth looking into

Vendor bank account change

A vendor’s deposit account changed between payments. It can be routine, but it’s also how payment fraud often looks, so confirm it with the vendor.

  • Same vendor
  • Different bank account
05 of 08 · Worth looking into

Unusually large payment

A payment was well above what this vendor is normally paid. It could be a real one-off, or a pricing or keying mistake.

  • Same vendor’s history
  • Well above typical
06 of 08 · Worth looking into

Invoice number shared across vendors

The same invoice number appears under two different vendors. It can be a coincidence, or a sign that a bill was recorded under the wrong vendor.

  • Same invoice number
  • Different vendor
07 of 08 · Discounts you could have had

Unclaimed early-payment discount

You paid inside the discount window, but still paid full price. It reads the payment terms on the invoice, like 2/10 net 30.

  • Paid within the window
  • Discount not applied
08 of 08 · Discounts you could have had

Missed early-payment discount

The terms offered a discount, but the invoice was paid after the window closed. It’s a process to change next time, not money already lost.

  • Discount was available
  • Paid after it closed
Before you trust a result

A flag isn’t money owed.

Reclaim only sees your file

A partial payment, a deposit or a credit outside your export can look like a mistake, so you check each result against your bank and your bills.

What every file needs

Vendor, payment date and amount paid. Invoice number, invoice amount, terms and the last four digits of the vendor’s bank account make more checks possible.

Where the checks run

Right in your browser, as soon as you add the file.
