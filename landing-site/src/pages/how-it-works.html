From your payments to your money back.

Upload an export of your payments. Reclaim checks every one, shows you the records behind each result, and helps you get the money back once you confirm it. Nothing reaches a vendor until you approve it.

Try it on sample data See how it works
01 Upload Your payments go in as a CSV.
02 Check Every payment gets compared.
03 Confirm You check the proof and decide.
04 Recover You approve, and track the money.
Step 1 of 6

Upload your payments

Export your payments as a CSV from QuickBooks Online, Xero, BILL, NetSuite or a spreadsheet. Our guides show which reports to run. Then log in, click Upload a ledger and drop the file in.

Step 2 of 6

Reclaim checks every payment

It compares each payment with the ones around it, looking for duplicates, overpayments, missed discounts, changed bank details and unusual amounts. The checks run right in your browser.

Step 3 of 6

You see the proof

Each finding shows the vendor, the amount and the exact payments behind it, with the matching details highlighted. A flag isn’t money owed. It means something is worth checking.

Step 4 of 6

You confirm it’s real

A few plain questions check the finding against your bank and your bills: did both payments clear, has a credit already covered it? Then you mark it real, ask for more detail, or dismiss it.

Step 5 of 6

You approve the request

Reclaim drafts the request from the records. You set the amount, choose a refund or a credit, and edit the message. Nothing goes out until you approve it, and you send it from your own email.

Step 6 of 6

You track it until the money lands

Recoveries shows where every request stands and when to follow up. You log the vendor’s replies, and money only counts as recovered when it actually comes back.
