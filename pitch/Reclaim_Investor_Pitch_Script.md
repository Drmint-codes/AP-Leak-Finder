# Reclaim Investor Pitch — Speaking Script

## 1. Reclaim

Hi, we’re Reclaim. We build supplier payment recovery software for small finance teams. When a business pays a supplier by mistake, spotting it is only the start. Reclaim handles the work after that: checking the records, preparing a supplier request the customer approves, and tracking it until the case is closed.

## 2. Problem: a payment mistake creates more work

Picture a small distributor with a $3,200 supplier bill. It gets paid twice, so $6,400 leaves the account, and $3,200 of that is extra cash tied up. But seeing two rows in a spreadsheet doesn’t prove what happened. Someone still has to prove the error, rule out a refund or a void, contact the supplier, and track whether the money ever comes back. In a small finance team, that work usually has no owner. We’re testing whether it happens often enough, and costs enough, that teams will pay us to handle it.

## 3. Solution: one place to finish each case

Reclaim keeps the mistake and all the work to resolve it in one place. The source records stay attached to the case. The software checks what’s missing and calculates the amount the records actually support, here a $3,200 request. The customer approves before any supplier is contacted. AI explains the facts and drafts the message. And the outcome, a refund, an applied credit or a clear explanation, stays linked to the case. To be clear, this is an illustrative example, not a customer result yet.

## 4. Why now: AI can reduce the manual work

Recovery services already exist, but they’re built for big companies because the work is manual and expensive. Two things change that today. Accounting APIs can now link bills, payments and credits directly. And AI tools can read documents and draft messages from approved facts. Put those together with fixed financial rules and customer approval, and small cases finally become worth pursuing. That’s exactly what our pilots will test.

## 5. Product: from CSV to a closed case

Here’s the workflow in five steps. Import: the customer uploads the CSV export they already have. Check: our rules flag possible errors. Evidence: Reclaim verifies the facts, like settlement, the bill and any adjustments, or asks for the missing records. Approve: the customer approves the supplier request. Close: we record the refund, the credit, or the explanation. Today our prototype covers CSV review and manual case tracking. Next are connected accounting records, a grounded AI assistant, and approved sending. And Reclaim never moves customer money.

## 6. Market: U.S. wholesale businesses

We’re starting with U.S. wholesale businesses. The Census counted 277,932 wholesale employer firms in 2022; that’s our ceiling. We assume about 20 percent of the firms with $2.5 to $25 million in receipts are a fit, which gives roughly 17,253 businesses. Our first goal is 100 customers. At our $50 Flat price, that’s a $166.8 million ceiling, a $10.4 million serviceable market, and $60,000 a year at 100 customers, before any recovery fees from our Growth plan.

## 7. Traction: prototype built, discovery started

Here’s where we are. We’ve built a working CSV review and recovery-case prototype. We won first place for best tech stack at an AI fintech hackathon. And we’ve started customer discovery with our first exploratory business call. We’re pre-revenue and haven’t verified a customer recovery yet, so our next milestone is three paid pilots that test real records, the effort to finish a case, and whether customers keep using Reclaim.

## 8. Business model: three simple plans

Our pricing is built so a business can start for free. The Free plan lets anyone upload their payment CSV and see their three lowest-value findings in full, enough to see the problem is real on their own books. Growth is $15 a month plus 10 percent of the money that actually comes back, so customers pay very little until Reclaim delivers. Flat is $50 a month with no recovery fee at all. Both paid plans include unlimited uploads, every finding in full, evidence with the source rows, AI-drafted supplier requests, approvals and follow-ups, partial returns, full case history and reports. Once a business recovers more than $350 a month, Flat becomes the cheaper option.

## 9. Go-to-market: pilots, then accountant referrals

Our first buyer is a distributor’s controller or AP lead who has a recent supplier-payment case they haven’t resolved. We’ll reach them directly in three steps: qualify a recent case, run a scoped pilot on their own records, and convert to a subscription once the value is clear. We’ll measure every step rather than assume a funnel. Once pilots work, accountants and fractional CFOs become our expansion channel, because each one can introduce many suitable clients.

## 10. Competition: where Reclaim aims to fit

There are four groups in this space. Books and review tools like QuickBooks, Xero, Xenett and Dext keep the books and run checks. Recovery services like PRGX and AP Impact send auditors after recoveries for large companies. AP platforms like Xelix, AppZen and OpenEnvoy focus on controls and automation. And claim software like AuditRes and RecoverAudit handle evidence and claims. Reclaim targets small finance teams with simple case completion. What we have to prove is easy setup, supported claims and clear outcomes. The moat we’re building is trusted integrations, case history and accountant adoption.
